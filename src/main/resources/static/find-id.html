Before kick-off

Find your team ID.

Every FPL team has a number. FPL Lab needs it to load your squad. Finding it takes under a minute.

fantasy.premierleague.com/entry/1234567/event/13

Your ID is the number straight after /entry/. Here it would be 1234567.

On your phone

Premier League app

The app doesn't display your team ID, so you'll need your phone's browser for this one step. You only have to do it once.

  1. Open your browserSafari, Chrome or any other, and go to fantasy.premierleague.com.
  2. Sign inUse the same email and password as the Premier League app.
  3. Tap PointsOpen your Points page.
  4. Read the addressTap the address bar to see the full link and find the number after /entry/. You can also copy the whole link and paste it below.
On a computer

FPL website

Quickest if you're at a laptop or desktop.

  1. Go to the FPL siteOpen fantasy.premierleague.com and sign in.
  2. Click PointsIt's in the menu next to Pick Team.
  3. Check the address barThe number after /entry/ is your team ID.

Stoppage time

Common questions

Is my ID the same every season?

No. You get a new team, and a new ID, when you sign up for a new season. If last season's number stops working, look it up again.

Do I have to sign in to FPL Lab?

No. Your ID only lets FPL Lab read your public team page, the same one your mini-league rivals can see. It can't make transfers or change anything on your account.

Can I look up a friend's or rival's team?

Yes. In FPL, open a mini-league and click their team name. Their ID is the number after /entry/ in that address.

Will FPL Lab remember my ID?

Yes, on this device. The last ID you entered is filled in for you next time.
