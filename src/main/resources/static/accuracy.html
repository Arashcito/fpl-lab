What do these numbers mean?
  • Average error: how far off a prediction is, on average, for players who played. Lower is better. FPL points are very random, so even a good model is about 2 points off.
  • Ranking: does the model put players in the right order? 1.00 = perfect order, 0 = random. This matters most for picking players.
  • Top 10 picks: average real points of the 10 players the model rated highest. Higher is better. This is what you would have got by trusting it.
  • Captain: the single highest-predicted player, and what he actually scored.
  • Bias: above 0 means the model was too optimistic, below 0 too pessimistic.
